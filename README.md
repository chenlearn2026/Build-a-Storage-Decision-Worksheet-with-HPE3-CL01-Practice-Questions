# Build-a-Storage-Decision-Worksheet-with-HPE3-CL01-Practice-Questions
The HPE3-CL01 Storage Portfolio Exam validates understanding of the Storage Portfolio coursework delivered through HPE Tech Pro continuous learning. Use CertQueen [HPE3-CL01 exam questions](https://www.certqueen.com/HPE3-CL01.html) alongside your training notes to identify which concepts you can apply and which lessons need another review.

A decision worksheet gives portfolio knowledge a practical setting and makes your reasoning easier to check.

## Exam details for your worksheet review

- **Delivery** - Web based, in English
- **Duration** - 60 minutes
- **Items** - 30 single-response multiple-choice questions
- **Passing score** - 75%

## Map the exercise to the official objective

HPE assigns **100%** of the exam to understanding the Storage Portfolio learning path materials. Complete the required training listed under the continuous learning badge and use that material to check the explanations behind your answers.

HPE lists preparation resources for **HPE ATP - Storage Solutions** and **HPE ATP - Edge-to-cloud architect**. These curricula put portfolio learning in a broader certification context. The activities below are study exercises you can adapt to examples in the required training.

## Start a worksheet from one course example

Choose a storage example from your HPE Tech Pro training. Record the workload, the customer's requirement, the option described in the course, and the explanation for choosing it. Keep a reference to the relevant lesson in your personal notes.

## Test the decision with a second requirement

Change one condition in the example, such as the expected growth or the required operating window. Decide whether you need a different option or additional information. Ask a colleague to review the worksheet and identify which statement actually supports your recommendation.

## Check what justifies the choice

After a practice question, identify the detail that guided your answer. Explain which different condition would make another choice reasonable. Change one fact in the scenario and work through the decision again.

Keep a short log of the concept, the lesson to revisit, and an example that would show you understand it. A correct answer is easier to build on when you can describe why it fits. If you repeatedly hesitate on the same distinction, return to the course explanation and try a fresh example after a break.

## Use the worksheet in further study

The associated HPE ATP learning paths help candidates build foundational understanding of solutions and their fit for customer needs. Storage portfolio knowledge gives those discussions a shared vocabulary and a set of examples to draw on.
